Our Story

Every name starts somewhere.

The Name

Gald3

The name is ours — Galde, spelled out for the internet. The “3” isn't a hidden meaning or a family crest detail; it's what was left once the obvious versions of the name were already taken. We kept it, because it was ours, and because it looked right set in a monogram.

That's the kind of honesty this site is built on. Not a manufactured legacy — a real one, still being written.

The Builder

Michael Galde

Right now, this site is kept by one person. Michael works in cybersecurity — research and teaching at the University of Arizona, along with the kind of projects that get built simply because they're worth building. The professional side of that work, consulting, speaking, research, lives at michaelgalde.com.

Gald3 is the other side of it — not a résumé, not a pitch. The foundation the rest of the family will eventually build on.

What Comes Next

Room for everyone who carries the name

The plan is simple, even if it's slow. As the rest of the family grows into needing their own place on the internet — an email address, a page of their own, a record worth keeping — there will be room for them here, under the same name, on the same domain.

“No rush. Good things built to last don't need to happen all at once.”
